# Prompt for Nothing Essential Apps

Upload the two reference images in the Essential Apps builder, then paste this prompt:

```text
Build a resizable Nothing Essential App home-screen calendar widget named Dayline. Use the first uploaded image as the app logo and the second as the visual reference. Let the launcher resize it horizontally and vertically; adapt cleanly from a compact 2×2 tile to a 2×4 widget. In 2×2 prioritize the date, centered month/year/time, and sunrise/sunset. Reveal the week strip and today's agenda as it grows. Never clip text or controls. This must be a compact, glanceable home-screen widget, not a full-screen mobile app.

Design: black background, thin charcoal outline, rounded corners, white typography, muted blue-gray metadata, and one small Nothing-red accent. Keep the quiet monochrome Nothing look. Use Inter throughout for a consistent font, including the date, month, year, clock, labels, and agenda. At the top, show the tiny calendar logo and DAYLINE label, with a refresh control. Show the current local weekday and large date on the left; center the month, year, and live local time exactly at the widget's horizontal midpoint; show sunrise and sunset times in a compact right column. Add a compact Sunday–Saturday date strip with today highlighted in a white pill. Under a thin divider, show TODAY and a Google Calendar link/action. Fit the next few items for today below it. Use small, readable event rows with time and title; use an all-day label for all-day items. If there are no items, show a calm empty state.

Behavior: request read-only on-device Calendar permission and show today's events from the user's synced Google Calendar. Request the device's Location permission to calculate local sunrise and sunset, show both times in the right column, and refresh them when the displayed date changes. Do not send or persist coordinates. Refresh the list when the widget refresh button is tapped and when the displayed date changes. Keep the current local date and time accurate. If the builder's Calendar integration exposes Google Tasks due today, show those beneath calendar events with a small task marker. Do not write to the calendar or persist event details. If calendar or location permission is denied, show a clear permission message and a retry action. Make the Google Calendar action open the selected day in Google Calendar when app links are supported.
```

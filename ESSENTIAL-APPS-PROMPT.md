# Prompt for Nothing's Essential Apps builder

Attach `logo.svg` as the logo/reference image. Add a screenshot of the web preview if you have one — the repository does not ship one. Then paste this prompt:

```text
Build a resizable Nothing Essential App home-screen calendar widget named Dayline. Use the uploaded image as the app logo and visual reference. Let the launcher resize it horizontally and vertically; adapt cleanly from a compact 2×2 tile to a 2×4 widget. In 2×2 prioritize the date, centered month/year/time, and sunrise/sunset. Reveal the week strip and today's agenda as it grows. Never clip text or controls. This must be a compact, glanceable home-screen widget, not a full-screen mobile app.

Design: black background, thin charcoal outline, rounded corners, white typography, muted blue-gray metadata, and one small Nothing-red accent. Keep the quiet monochrome Nothing look. Use one family throughout — Inter if available, otherwise the system font — for the date, month, year, clock, labels and agenda. At the top, show the tiny calendar logo and DAYLINE label, with a refresh control. Show the current local weekday and large date on the left; center the month, year and live local time exactly at the widget's horizontal midpoint; show sunrise and sunset times in a compact right column. Add a compact Sunday–Saturday date strip: today is a filled white pill, and if another day is selected it gets a thin white ring instead. Put small ‹ › buttons at the ends of the strip to move a week at a time. Under a thin divider, show TODAY (or the selected day's name) and a Google Calendar action. Fit the next few items for today below it. Use small, readable event rows with time and title; use an all-day label for all-day items; mark tasks with a small square marker. If there are no items, show a calm empty state.

Behavior: request read-only on-device Calendar permission. On first use, show a short in-widget explanation of what is read and a Connect action, then trigger the system permission prompt — never a dead error. Request Location permission to calculate local sunrise and sunset, show both times in the right column, and recalculate them when the displayed date changes. Do not send or persist coordinates. Refresh the list when the refresh control is tapped and when the displayed date or week changes. Keep the local date and time accurate, and roll over to the new day at midnight without resetting a day the user is browsing. Do not write to the calendar or persist event details. If calendar or location permission is denied, keep the date and sun times usable and show a clear message with a Retry action. Make the Google Calendar action open the selected day in Google Calendar when app links are supported. If Tasks are available, show tasks due on the selected day beneath calendar events.
```
